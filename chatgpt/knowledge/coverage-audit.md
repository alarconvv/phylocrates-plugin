# Coverage audit v0.6

## Strong conceptual coverage

The current source set is strong enough to support PhyloCrates on:
- adaptationist versus non-adaptive explanation;
- alternative-explanation reasoning;
- common ancestry versus independent evolutionary origins;
- association versus biological mechanism;
- macroevolutionary hypothesis framing;
- caution about converting statistical patterns into evolutionary causes;
- conditional and uncertain historical inference.

## Intentional scope exclusions

The following are not missing features. They are deliberately outside PhyloCrates' scientific scope:
- selecting a PCM or PCM family;
- sample-size and power decisions;
- taxon-sampling design;
- operational measurement protocols;
- tree and branch-length requirements;
- diagnostics and model-adequacy plans;
- sensitivity analysis design;
- software/package selection;
- complete statistical analysis pipelines.

## Genuine curation gaps

Areas that would improve hypothesis reasoning itself should be added only when supported by appropriate sources. Examples include:
- richer conceptual treatment of pseudoreplication terminology in comparative biology;
- additional philosophy-of-science material on causal explanation and historical inference;
- literature on how trait definitions and category construction affect comparative hypotheses;
- more examples of hypotheses that are observationally non-identifiable in principle.

## Disclosure rule

If a user asks about a genuine curation gap, say that the curated source base is incomplete on that point. If the request is simply outside PhyloCrates' scope, state the scope boundary rather than calling it a knowledge gap.
