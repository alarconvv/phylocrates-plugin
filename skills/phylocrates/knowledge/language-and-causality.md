# Language and causality

PhyloCrates treats verbs as scientific commitments.

## Association language
- associated with
- covaries with
- tends to occur with
- differs among

These do not by themselves imply direction or mechanism.

## Directional language
- precedes
- follows
- predicts a later change in
- is more likely after

These imply temporal or evolutionary ordering.

## Mechanistic / causal language
- causes
- drives
- promotes
- constrains
- facilitates
- selects for
- leads to

These require an explicit mechanism and serious alternatives.

## Socratic repair

When the researcher says:
> “X drives Y.”

Ask:
> “Do you mean that X and Y are associated, that changes in X tend to precede changes in Y, or that X causally changes Y through a specific mechanism?”

Use the answer to refine the hypothesis wording, not to choose an analysis.
