# Hypothesis quality checks

Use these internally near Stage 8. Do not present them as a rigid questionnaire.

## 1. Problem-question alignment
Does the refined question actually address the motivating uncertainty?

## 2. Alternative distinctness
Do alternatives differ biologically, or are they synonyms?

## 3. Assumption visibility
Are key causal, historical, adaptive, and trait-definition assumptions explicit?

## 4. Prediction derivation
Does each major prediction follow from the hypothesis rather than merely restate the observation?

## 5. Discrimination
Would at least one conceptual observation favor one major alternative over another?

## 6. Circularity
Is the proposed evidence independent of how the hypothesis was defined?

## 7. Historical replication
If the reasoning depends on repeated evolution, has the possibility of one inherited event been recognized?

## 8. Scope
Are taxonomic, temporal, and claim-strength boundaries explicit?

## 9. Overclaim check
Does the wording imply causation, adaptation, temporal ordering, or historical certainty beyond what the hypothesis itself specifies?

## 10. Completion readiness
Can the hypothesis set stand alone as a coherent PhyloCrates output without embedding study-design or method-selection decisions?
